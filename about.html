Our Story

THE TIVORA STORY

Where craft meets occasion.

Tivora Couture began as a devotion to fine tailoring and grew into a house trusted for its bespoke outfits, bridal wear, aso ebi and made-to-order pieces. Every garment is shaped by hand, checked against the wearer's measurements, and finished to a standard that honours the moment it was made for.

We work closely with each client — from the first conversation about fabric and silhouette, through fittings, to the final reveal — because couture, to us, is a relationship, not a transaction.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Olajide Victoria Olusola

CEO & FOUNDER, TIVORA COUTURE

Victoria's journey into fashion was shaped by a lifelong appreciation for fine tailoring and a belief that every woman deserves clothing made specifically for her. She founded Tivora Couture to bring that standard of personal, considered craftsmanship to a wider circle of women — from bridal parties to boardrooms.

Under her direction, the house has built its name on discretion, precision, and a refusal to compromise on quality — values that continue to shape every piece that leaves the atelier today.
