HOW TO MEASURE

Taking Your Own Measurements

01

Use a soft measuring tape and wear fitted clothing for accuracy.

02

Bust — measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.

03

Waist — measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline.

04

Hips — measure around the fullest part of your hips, roughly 8 inches below the waist.
